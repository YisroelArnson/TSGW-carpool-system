# Parent Tutorial: Using TSGW Carpool

This guide is for parents and guardians using the TSGW carpool system for pickup.

The main idea is simple: when you are ready for pickup, you tell the school which child or children you are here for. The classroom display updates, and the school knows who should be sent out.

Parents do not need to install an app or create a password. You use the website and your Family Number.

## Before You Begin

You will need:

- The parent pickup website link.
- Your Family Number from the school.
- A phone, tablet, or computer with internet access.

If you are picking up another family's child, that child's parent must give your family pickup permission first.

## 1. Sign In With Your Family Number

1. Open the parent pickup website.
2. Find the box labeled `Family Number`.
3. Enter your Family Number, for example `101`.
4. Tap `Continue`.

After you enter your Family Number successfully, the page will remember it on that device. The next time you open the page, it will bring you straight to your family pickup screen.

If you need to use a different Family Number, tap `Log out` at the top of the page and enter the correct number.

## 2. Check In for Pickup

Use check-in when you are ready for the school to send your child or children out.

1. Open the parent pickup website.
2. If asked, enter your Family Number and tap `Continue`.
3. On the `Who are you picking up today?` screen, tap each child you are picking up.
4. If you are picking up all of your children, tap `Select All`.
5. At the bottom of the screen, tap the `I'm Here` button. The button will show how many children you selected.

The school is notified right away. The selected child or children will show as called on the classroom display.

### If You Made a Mistake

If you selected the wrong child, use the cancel button next to that child's pickup request if it is still available.

If the child is still not outside after a few minutes, use `Call Again`. This reminds the classroom again. You may need to wait a short time before calling again.

## 3. Send a Pickup Request Later

If you are close to school but not there yet, you can set a timer.

1. Choose the child or children you want picked up.
2. Tap the clock button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Choose how many minutes to wait.
4. Tap `Start Timer`.

The request will be sent automatically when the timer ends. You can cancel the timer before it sends.

## 4. Add a Saved Carpool

A saved carpool is a quick pick for a group of students you pick up together often. For example, you might create `Monday Carpool` for the children you take home every Monday.

1. Sign in with your Family Number.
2. Tap the settings icon at the top of the page.
3. In `Saved Carpools`, tap `Add New Carpool`.
4. Enter a `Carpool Name`, such as `Monday Carpool`.
5. Choose the days of the week when you usually use this carpool.
6. Choose the students who belong in this saved carpool.
7. Tap `Save Carpool`.

The saved carpool will now appear as a quick pick on the daily check-in page. When you need it, tap the saved carpool, then tap the `I'm Here` button at the bottom.

You can edit or delete a saved carpool from Parent Settings.

## 5. Understand Pickup Permissions

Pickup permissions control which families are allowed to pick up which children.

There are two important sections in Parent Settings:

- `Students You Can Pick Up`: children that other parents have allowed your family to pick up.
- `Who Can Pick Up Your Children`: families that you have allowed to pick up your children.

If you need to pick up another family's child, that child's parent must add your family under `Who Can Pick Up Your Children` on their settings page.

## 6. Give Another Family Permission to Pick Up Your Child

Use this when another parent should be allowed to pick up one or more of your children.

1. Sign in with your Family Number.
2. Tap the settings icon at the top of the page.
3. Go to `Who Can Pick Up Your Children`.
4. Tap `Add Permission`.
5. Search for the other family by parent name.
6. Tap the correct family.
7. Choose which of your children that family can pick up.
8. Choose the start date.
9. Choose an end date, or check `Keep this permission until I remove it`.
10. Tap `Save Permission`.

That family can now check in for the child or children you selected.

You can edit or remove the permission later from the same section.

## 7. Get Permission to Pick Up Another Family's Child

You cannot give yourself permission to pick up someone else's child. The other child's parent must give permission from their own Parent Settings.

Ask the other parent to:

1. Open their Parent Settings.
2. Go to `Who Can Pick Up Your Children`.
3. Tap `Add Permission`.
4. Search for your family.
5. Choose the child or children you may pick up.
6. Save the permission.

After they save it, that child will appear for you in:

- `Students You Can Pick Up` in Parent Settings.
- `Also Approved For Pickup` on your daily check-in page.

## Troubleshooting

### I entered my Family Number, but it did not work.

Check that the number is typed correctly. Use numbers only. If it still does not work, contact the school office.

### I do not see another family's child.

That child's parent may not have given your family permission yet, or the permission may have expired. Ask the parent to check their Parent Settings.

### I made a saved carpool, but a student is missing.

A student can only be added if they are your child or if you currently have pickup permission for that student. Check your permissions first, then edit the saved carpool.

### I am using someone else's phone.

Tap `Log out` when you are done so the phone does not stay connected to your Family Number.

Pickup permissions let another family pick up your child, or let your family pick up another parent's child.
