Troubleshooting

Common pickup questions

I entered my Family Number, but it did not work.

Check that the number is typed correctly. Use numbers only. If it still does not work, contact the school office.

I do not see another family's child.

That child's parent may not have given your family permission yet, or the permission may have expired. Ask the parent to check Parent Settings.

I made a saved carpool, but a student is missing.

A student can only be added if they are your child or if you currently have pickup permission for that student.

I am using someone else's phone.

Tap Log out when you are done so the phone does not stay connected to your Family Number.

I don't have a smartphone. Can I still use the system?

Yes. There is still a spotter in the parking lot. Tell the spotter your Family Number, and they can check you in.

What happens if a child asks for a ride?

A child should only be sent with another family when pickup permission is already approved. Ask that child's parent to grant your family permission before pickup.
